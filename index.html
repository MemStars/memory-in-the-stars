Frequently Asked Questions

Do I actually own a star?

No. This is a commemorative registration that pairs your dedication with the coordinates of a real star from a public astronomical catalog. It does not create legal ownership or an official astronomical name.

Are the coordinates real?

Yes. Each registration is linked to a real star listed in a publicly available astronomical catalog and includes its Right Ascension and Declination.

Is this affiliated with NASA?

No. Memory in the Stars is an independent commemorative gift service and is not affiliated with NASA or the International Astronomical Union (IAU).
